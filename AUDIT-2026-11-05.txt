Main entities:

- customers: contact details and creator/editor references; phone is indexed, not unique.
- destinations and rates: one rate per destination for legacy rated bookings.

Local sample size is small: 3 customers, 3 shipments, 3 dispatches, 3 payment entries, 9 storage movements and no open entry drafts. It cannot prove performance at operational scale.

3. EXISTING CAPABILITIES TO PRESERVE
Authentication: session login, hashed passwords, login throttle, CSRF, session regeneration, active-user checks and password/session invalidation. No public registration.
Permissions: only admin and staff exist in schema, validation and User::isAdmin. Staff currently have customers, parcels, storage, trips, payments, reports/exports and reconciliation. Admin additionally has settings, users/rates/methods and refunds. Preserve backend checks; hiding navigation is not authorization.
Branch operations: BranchAccess, visibleTo scopes and service checks govern readable records and responsible operation branches. Admins select the operating branch on Home. Do not erase that explicit context in a simplified interface.
Booking: walk-in customer creation and parcel storage receipt are transactional; customer reuse currently requires matching name, phone and address. Server generates tracking and saves sender, currency and charge snapshots. Booking-key replay rejects changed details and other employees.
Search: parcel tracking, sender/recipient name and phone; customer name/phone; trip carrier/flight/reference/parcel; storage location and parcel/customer names. Preserve server-side search rather than substituting a filter over only the displayed page.
Photos: private storage, authenticated access, type/size/dimension limits, up to five photos, duplicate fingerprints, optional image optimization/orientation handling and cleanup on failure. Preserve saved draft photos.
Drafts: save/reopen incomplete bookings and trips, branch checks, revision conflict handling and completion links. Back/Next must retain fields, photos, booking/draft keys and revision.
Dispatch: eligible stored origin parcels only; one route per dispatch; flight and origin-timezone departure; draft plans, unassignment before handover, manifest printing and physical handover. Quick dispatch advances preparation stages using existing services.
Destination receipt: arrival, storage receipt/location and Ready for Collection are coordinated transactionally.
Collection: correct destination operation branch, stored parcel, eligible status, collector name and settled balance. Optional outstanding payment and collection occur in one transaction. Repeat completed collection returns without creating a second handover.
Storage: receipt/move/release histories; archive/restore does not remove physical stock or parcel/payment history; cancelled parcels require physical return recording to release storage.
Records: receipt/label and manifest printing, payment history, status history, branch queues, collected records and customer history.
Reports: charge/receipt/refund/net/outstanding totals, CSV exports with BOM/formula protection, reconciliation snapshots and stale-ledger detection.
Settings: company/receipt details, legacy rates, payment methods, staff assignment/deactivation and last-active-admin protection.
PWA/mobile: install controls, offline explanation, safe-area navigation, reduced-motion support and public-asset caching. Authenticated pages, photos and ledger data are deliberately not cached for offline operations.

4. FINANCIAL AND STATUS INVARIANTS
Sources: app/Services/CourierService.php, SimpleWorkflowService.php, Money.php, PaymentDetails.php, ReportService.php, BranchAccess.php; app/Models/Shipment.php and Payment.php.
- New branch bookings use a positive manually agreed charge in MMK or SGD and kg. Do not replace this with legacy automatic destination-rate pricing.
- Legacy pricing is max(minimum, base + upward-rounded-to-cent(weight * per_weight)) + additional charge. Quantity is descriptive and does not multiply that total-weight charge.
- Individual money inputs use integer minor-unit arithmetic and decimal database fields; BCMath handles fractional weights. Do not introduce floating-point financial calculations.
- Saved price/currency and sender/rate snapshots are preserved; correction uses existing cancellation/rebooking/refund workflows, not editing past ledger entries.
- Payment must be positive and no larger than the current balance; inactive methods and cancelled parcels cannot receive new payments. Payment replay validates transaction details and receiving branch.
- Cash tendered and change are distinct from the applied payment. Bank transfer and Pay currently require a provider remark; change is Cash-only.
- Refunds are admin-only, linked to an original payment, bounded by its unrefunded amount, and retain its method and receiving branch.
- Charges and outstanding belong to the origin branch; receipts/refunds belong to the payment branch. Currencies are reported separately; no conversion or branch remittance accounting exists.
- Outstanding is a current all-dates balance, including cancelled bookings awaiting review. Booked charges exclude cancelled parcels. Neither is profit or revenue recognition.
- Reconciliation uses net receipts by branch/currency/date/method, supports negative actuals, requires variance notes and retains a ledger version. Do not replace stored snapshots with live mutable summaries.
- Operational writes use transactions and row locks. Retain duplicate/replay protection, transaction boundaries and deterministic lock order.
- Branch flow: Received -> Processing -> Ready for Dispatch -> physical handover/Dispatched -> optional In Transit -> Arrived at Branch -> Ready for Collection -> Collected. Legacy Booked/Delivered paths remain supported.
- Assigned draft parcels must be unassigned before returning to warehouse stages/cancellation. Handed-over dispatches cannot be undone in the interface. Exceptions/cancellations need reasons; destination arrival/collection require recorded handover.

5. VERIFIED UX GAPS AND DEFECTS
Priority here is redesign order, not a claim of measured user-study severity.

HIGH: Booking is one long multi-section form rather than a guided task. It exposes branch choices, sender/recipient, storage/date/photos, weights and financial fields together. No dedicated review step exists.
Evidence: resources/views/shipments/create.blade.php.
Action: add customer -> recipient/destination -> parcel -> review steps over the same validated server submission, retaining drafts and existing services.

HIGH: Home mixes promotional copy, recent records, workload, queue counts and financial information. The four requested task actions are not presented together; financial reporting is visible to both existing roles.
Evidence: resources/views/dashboard.blade.php; DashboardController invokes ReportService::summary, which loads all outstanding shipments/payments for the selected scope.
Action: task-first Home with four real workflow entries and small operational counts; keep full financial records reachable under More. Stop loading full financial summaries if the simplified Home does not display them.

HIGH: Later CSS overrides shrink the interface below the requested reading sizes. In public/css/theme.css the later screen block sets body 14px, button 13px, labels 14px, help 12px, table cells 13px and badges 11px. Base component rules also allow small controls.
Evidence: public/css/theme.css, especially the screen block beginning around line 611; public/css/app.css; Burmese/mobile overrides.
Action: consolidate the cascade into shared readable typography/spacing tokens; verify computed sizes in both languages and at mobile widths. Do not only change the first body rule, because later rules override it.

HIGH: The quick-send form can physically dispatch in the same submission used for trip preparation without a separate selection review/confirmation. Customer collection similarly lacks the requested explicit confirmation. Backend eligibility and payment checks exist and must remain.
Evidence: resources/views/dispatches/index.blade.php; resources/views/shipments/workflow.blade.php. In contrast dispatches/show.blade.php already has data-confirm for handover and shipments/show.blade.php has it for refunds.
Action: clear review with parcel/recipient/flight details and explicit confirmation; reuse existing transaction endpoints. Preserve JavaScript navigation.js duplicate-submit guard and server replay/locking behavior.

MEDIUM: No phone-based existing-customer picker is exposed on booking. Backend accepts customer_id, but the form only has customer_name/phone/address. Matching all three exact strings can create duplicate customers when contact formatting/address differs.
Evidence: ShipmentController::store; CourierService::book; shipments/create.blade.php; customers.phone non-unique index.
Action: search and explicitly choose a matching customer; handle multiple people sharing a phone. Do not silently merge customers or make phone globally unique.

MEDIUM: Navigation leads with Parcel storage, Carrier trips, Bookkeeping; mobile uses Storage, Trips, Books. Receive/Send/Find are not persistent primary task labels. Arrow-only row links remain visually ambiguous even when accessible labels exist.
Evidence: layouts/app.blade.php, components/mobile-workspace.blade.php, shipments/table.blade.php.
Action: shared task labels for Home, Receive a Parcel, Send Parcels, Find a Parcel, More; preserve all secondary destinations and admin-only gates.

MEDIUM: Missing Myanmar literal translation keys: Sign in, Close, Date / time. These occur on the active login, PWA help and storage-history templates.
Evidence: lang/my.json compared with literal __() calls in app and resources/views; auth/login.blade.php, components/pwa-controls.blade.php, inventory/index.blade.php and inventory/parcel.blade.php.
Action: translate these and add coverage for new workflow strings. Static key coverage does not certify natural Burmese wording, dynamic keys or font availability on Android/iOS.

MEDIUM: Language is stored in session and a 180-day browser cookie, not on the employee account. Shared browsers can carry the previous employee's language; cross-device per-employee persistence is absent.
Evidence: LanguageController, SetLocale, User fillable/schema.
Action: retain existing persistence during presentation changes. If cross-device employee preferences are required, propose an additive migration separately after this audit; no migration is needed for the first interface changes.

MEDIUM: /audit is a registered admin-only route with an existing template, but SettingsController::audit unconditionally aborts with 404. History continues to be written; the administrative audit viewer is unavailable.
Evidence: routes/web.php; SettingsController::audit; resources/views/settings/audit.blade.php.
Action: establish whether the viewer was intentionally disabled, then restore only with the existing admin gate and protected pagination/redaction as appropriate. Do not remove stored audit history.

MEDIUM: Company currency validation contains a literal newline in the regex between {3} and $. A normal three-letter value therefore cannot match that expression, potentially blocking all company-details saves. The existing lock test expects a currency error and does not prove a valid company save succeeds.
Evidence: app/Http/Controllers/SettingsController.php lines 38-39; tests/Feature/CourierWorkflowTest.php settings-company assertions.
Action: isolated validation regression test and correction before redesigning settings; retain the separate first-booking currency/unit lock. No correction was made during this audit.

REVIEW: CustomerController::show loads customer shipment history without Shipment::visibleTo, while direct parcel pages do enforce canRead. Customer records are otherwise globally shared. This is an existing policy inconsistency to clarify for unrelated-branch and future-branch data, not permission to change current access rules.
Evidence: CustomerController::index/show/edit/update, resources/views/customers/show.blade.php and ShipmentController::show.

REVIEW: PaymentDetails and client display behavior depend on exact configurable names Cash, Bank transfer and Pay. Renaming them can change required remarks/change behavior. Presentational translations must preserve canonical names; replacing them with typed capabilities requires its own business/schema design.

6. SCOPE MISMATCHES REQUIRING EXPLICIT BUSINESS DESIGN
The brief's Cashier/Warehouse/Operations/Accountant roles do not exist. They can guide task presentation, but must not be invented as new authorized roles or inferred from employees' names. Actual role expansion requires a separately specified permission matrix, backend enforcement and migration plan.
No expense ledger, profit calculation, payroll, FX conversion or inter-branch remittance module exists. Do not create fake expense/income pages or describe net receipts as profit.
The current application is designed around the YGN/SG pair. A generic branch management screen or automatically guessed destination for additional branches would expand business scope.
Clarify whether any outstanding balance may be deferred at collection; the current implemented rule is full settlement. Until changed through verified business requirements, preserve it.
Clarify shared-phone/customer identity policy and whether /audit was intentionally disabled.
